## Studio Overview

The **Studio** panel (located on the right side of the workspace) provides one-click generation of seven structured study assets. Studio synthesizers read all currently enabled sources in your notebook to generate cohesive, multi-page study artifacts.

---

## The 7 Studio Synthesizers

### 1. Voice Audio Overview
- **What it does**: Generates a natural, podcast-style conversational script between two co-hosts—**Alex** and **Jordan**—who discuss, explain, and debate the central themes of your documents.
- **Audio Synthesis**:
  - **Google Gemini TTS**: High-fidelity natural voices (Puck, Kore, Charon, Aoede, Fenrir).
  - **OpenAI TTS**: Natural alloy, echo, fable, onyx, nova, and shimmer voices.
  - **Basic Voice (Free)**: Zero-key fallback powered entirely by your browser's local SpeechSynthesis API.

### 2. Practice Quiz
- Generates a standalone deck of 5 to 10 multiple-choice questions with answer keys, explanations, and citation links.

### 3. Spaced Recall Flashcards
- Generates interactive, flippable flashcards covering key definitions, mathematical theorems, or historical dates. You can flip each card and mark whether you remembered it.

### 4. Study Guide
- A formatted serif curriculum with essential terminology, key conceptual frameworks, sample exam prompts, and cross-source comparisons.

### 5. Executive Summary
- A concise briefing document featuring high-level takeaways, core arguments, and quantitative data tables.

### 6. Interactive Mind Map
- Visual, zoomable, and collapsible hierarchical concept graph rendered as an interactive node tree. Excellent for understanding complex taxonomies.

### 7. Formal Briefing Report
- An analytical academic essay with formal section headings, structured arguments, and full Chicago/APA citation footnotes.

---

## Exporting Studio Outputs

All generated studio outputs can be:
- Copied directly to your clipboard in formatted Markdown.
- Saved into your notebook's **Saved Notes** column for perpetual reference.
- Exported as formatted text or PDF documents.
