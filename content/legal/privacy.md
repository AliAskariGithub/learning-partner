## 2. Information We Collect

We adhere to the principle of data minimization. We only collect and store data necessary to deliver the grounded study features of Learning Partner:

### A. Account Details
When you sign up or log in via Neon Auth (including email authentication and Google OAuth), we store your authenticated user identifier, primary email address, display name, and avatar URL if provided.

### B. Notebooks, Sources, and Document Content
When you create a study notebook, we store the notebook metadata (title, instructions). When you upload documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD) or add public web links, we extract the textual content into discrete passages ("source chunks") and store their full-text search representations and embeddings in our database. Original files are stored in secured object storage.

### C. Chat Messages and Interaction Modes
We store your prompts, AI responses, citation references, and mode preferences (Chat, Learning, or Quiz) so your study sessions remain accessible whenever you log in.

### D. Study Images
In Learning Mode, you may upload photos of textbook pages, problem sets, or lecture slides. These images are validated, stripped of all EXIF/GPS metadata, and stored securely in object storage solely to allow vision-capable AI models to assist with your study questions.

### E. Shared Notebook Memory
Learning Partner features automated notebook memory consolidation. The system extracts study preferences, mastered concepts, and topics needing reinforcement, saving them to your notebook profile. You can review, edit, or purge memory items at any time in your notebook settings.

### F. Quiz Attempts and Scoring
When you take interactive quizzes generated by Studio or Quiz mode, we record your selected answers, percentage scores, time spent, and topic weaknesses to help you track retention.

### G. Encrypted API Keys and Connection Credentials
When you connect an AI provider using Bring Your Own Key (BYOK), your API keys are encrypted at rest using industry-standard **AES-256-GCM** with unique initialization vectors. Plaintext keys are never stored in databases, never written to server logs, and never exposed in browser developer tools.

### H. Contact and Feedback Inquiries
When you submit a message via `/contact` or a bug report via `/feedback`, we record your name, email, report details, optional screenshot, and client environment context (browser version, OS, current page URL) to troubleshoot issues.

### I. Anti-Spam Security Records
To defend public forms against distributed abuse and automated bots, we store a cryptographically salted **SHA-256 hash** of your client IP address. Raw IP addresses are never stored in our database. Salted IP hashes are permanently purged after 30 days.

### J. Cookies and Local Preferences
We use strictly necessary cookies to maintain your authenticated session. Optional theme preferences and UI language settings are stored locally in your browser per our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively to provide, maintain, and secure the study workspace:

1. **Grounded Question Answering**: To retrieve relevant chunks from your enabled sources and provide verifiable citation chips.
2. **Interactive Study Tools**: To generate flashcards, practice quizzes, study guides, and audio scripts based on your uploaded documents.
3. **Session Continuity**: To restore your open notebooks, chat history, and memory across devices.
4. **Platform Security**: To prevent Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF), detect automated form spam, and enforce rate limits.

**We never sell your personal information, and we do not use your documents, custom notes, or study chats to train AI models.**

## 4. Third-Party AI Providers

Learning Partner is a Bring Your Own AI (BYOK) application. Content you submit in chat or studio requests is transmitted to the AI provider corresponding to your active connection:

- **Google Gemini**: If you configure a Google Gemini API key or use Gemini TTS. Please note that under Google's published terms, requests made on the Google Gemini **Free Tier** may be processed and reviewed by Google systems to improve their products. If you require complete enterprise privacy, Google recommends their paid tier, or you may connect other providers.
- **OpenAI**: If you connect an OpenAI API key or use OpenAI TTS for voice synthesis. OpenAI's API data usage policies state that data sent via their commercial API is not used to train models.
- **Anthropic Claude**: If you connect an Anthropic API key. Anthropic does not train models on API inputs or outputs.
- **Groq & OpenRouter**: If you route requests through Groq LPUs or OpenRouter inference endpoints.
- **Custom Endpoints**: If you connect a self-hosted inference server (e.g. vLLM, Ollama, LocalAI). Calls remain within your configured network endpoint.

You are responsible for reviewing and agreeing to the privacy policies and service terms of your selected AI providers.

## 6. Data Retention and Self-Service Control

We retain your workspace content for as long as your account remains active. You maintain complete control over your data:

- **Self-Service Export**: You can download a complete, structured JSON export of all your notebooks, sources, notes, conversations, studio outputs, contact inquiries, and feedback reports from the [Settings](/settings) page at any time.
- **Permanent Account Deletion**: You can permanently delete your account directly from [Settings](/settings). This immediately and irrecoverably deletes your profile, sources, extracted chunks, chat messages, memory, saved notes, API keys, and authentication records from Neon Postgres and Neon Auth.
- **Log Retention**: Automated spam IP hashes are scrubbed automatically after 30 days. Contact and feedback records are archived after 365 days.

## 7. Security Safeguards

We implement modern technical and organizational safeguards:

- **Encryption in Transit**: Strict HTTPS/TLS enforcement across all endpoints.
- **Encryption at Rest**: AES-256-GCM key encryption with unique per-credential initialization vectors.
- **Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation**: Every database query is strictly partitioned and scoped by authenticated user ID.
- **SSRF Defenses**: Ingestion pipelines strictly block requests to private subnets (RFC 1918), loopback interfaces, link-local addresses, and cloud provider metadata services (169.254.169.254).
- **Upload Inspection**: File uploads are capped at 25MB, validated for permitted extensions, and screened for executable payloads. Image uploads undergo magic bytes validation and EXIF metadata stripping.

To learn more about our defensive posture, visit our dedicated [Security Overview](/security).
