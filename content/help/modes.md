## The Three Study Modes

Learning Partner provides three distinct operational modes, selectable from the pill menu in the chat bar:

```
[ Chat ]  [ Learn ]  [ Quiz ]
```

---

## 1. Chat Mode: Grounded Research Synthesis

- **Primary Goal**: Fast, objective, comprehensive synthesis of your enabled sources.
- **Tone**: Analytical, direct, and structured.
- **Citation Behavior**: Every statement drawn from your documents is anchored with a clickable citation chip (`[1]`, `[2]`).
- **Absence of Information**: If your question cannot be answered using the provided sources, the assistant will explicitly respond: *"I could not find information about that in your sources."*

---

## 2. Learning Mode: Socratic Tutor & Vision Uploads

- **Primary Goal**: Deep conceptual understanding through guided inquiry.
- **Tone**: Socratic, conversational tutor. Instead of providing the final answer immediately, the AI asks probing questions, breaks complex problems into logical steps, and encourages you to deduce the solution.
- **Vision Image Uploads**:
  - Click the **Paperclip** icon in the chat bar to attach up to 5 images per message.
  - Supported formats: **PNG, JPEG, WebP** up to 10MB each.
  - Ideal for photographing handwritten equations, textbook diagrams, laboratory apparatus charts, or practice exam questions.
  - *Capability Note*: Vision uploads require a vision-capable model (e.g., Gemini 3.8 Flash, GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet).

---

## 3. Quiz Mode: Active Recall with Live Scoring

- **Primary Goal**: Test your memory and identify knowledge gaps before exams.
- **Interactive Scoring**:
  - The model generates structured multiple-choice questions grounded in your sources.
  - You click an answer option to see instantaneous feedback.
  - Correct answers illuminate in green with supporting explanations and source citations.
  - Incorrect choices illuminate in rose with constructive feedback.
- **Retention Analytics**:
  - Quiz attempts, overall percentage scores, and question-by-question metrics are saved to your profile for long-term review.

---

## Switching Between Modes

You can switch modes at any time during an active study session. Your previous chat history and notebook memory remain preserved.
